# Home Page (Generating an Image)

✨ [日本語](home.md)

This page lets you generate a single image (or several copies of the same one). It's the best place to start.

![Home page](../images/dashboard_page_en.png)

## Steps

1. At the top of the page, choose a **Workflow** — the art style you want (e.g. `anima`, `sdxl`). The available options depend on what has been set up for you.
2. In the **Positive Prompt** box, type what you want to see, as English words or phrases separated by commas (e.g. `1girl, blue hair, smile`).
3. In the **Negative Prompt** box, type what you don't want to see, the same way (the text that's already there can usually stay as-is).
4. Choose an **Image Size** — vertical, horizontal, square, or a custom size.
5. If you like, add a **LoRA** — an optional add-on effect for more advanced users. It's fine to skip this if you're not sure what it does.
6. If you like, enter a **Filename Prefix** — text to add to the beginning of the saved image's filename. It's fine to leave this blank.
7. Choose how many images to generate at once with **Batch Count** (1–10).
8. Click the **Run** button at the bottom left.

## While and After It Runs

- A progress bar appears at the bottom of the screen while the image is being generated.
- Once it's done, thumbnails of the generated images appear on the right. Click one to view it larger.
- If something goes wrong, a red notification (error message) appears in the corner of the screen. Check what it says, and ask your administrator if needed.

## Saving and Reloading Settings (Import / Export)

Click **Export** at the top right to save everything you've entered (workflow, prompts, image size, etc.) to a file.
Click **Import** to load a saved file and restore those settings instantly — handy if you often generate similar images.
