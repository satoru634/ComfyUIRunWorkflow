# Data Page (Reviewing Your Results)

✨ [日本語](data.md)

This page lets you look back at every image you've generated, and every tagging result you've produced.

![Data page](../images/data_page_en.png)

## Generated Results Tab

- Shows every image generated from the [Home](home_english.md) or [Queue](queue_english.md) page, newest first.
- Click a row to see all the images generated in that run.
- Click a thumbnail to view it larger.

## Tag History Tab

- Shows every tagging result from the [Tagger page](tagger_english.md), newest first.
- Click **Copy** on any card to copy its tags to the clipboard.

## Refreshing the List

Click **Refresh** at the top right to reload both tabs with the latest results.
