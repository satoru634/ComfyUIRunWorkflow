# Tagger Page (Extracting Tags from an Image)

✨ [日本語](tagger.md)

Select an image, and this page automatically figures out words (tags) that describe what's in it.

![Tagger page](../images/tagger_page_en.png)

## Steps

1. Click **Select Image**, or drag and drop an image file onto the screen.
2. The selected image appears as a preview.
3. Click **Run Tagging**.
4. After a moment, the tags (comma-separated words) appear on the right.
5. Click **Copy** to copy the tags to your clipboard — you can paste them into another app.

## Good to Know

- The fine-tuning settings used for tagging (such as accuracy thresholds) are configured by your administrator ahead of time and can't be changed on this page.
- Every tagging result is saved automatically and can be reviewed anytime from the "Tag History" tab on the [Data page](data_english.md).
